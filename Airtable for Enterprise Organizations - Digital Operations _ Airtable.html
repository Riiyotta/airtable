Frequently asked questions

Most AI tools improve individual work but don't connect across teams, systems, or workflows. Airtable is the shared operational layer where humans and agents read, write, and build together — so intelligence compounds into durable work instead of disappearing when a session ends.

Every human and agent action — including work done through ChatGPT or Claude via MCP — is logged, inspectable, and governed by granular permissions down to the record and field level. Airtable is SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, and GDPR compliant, with SSO, SCIM, and org-wide AI policy management from a single Enterprise Hub.

Agents work alongside people, not instead of them. Airtable is built for human-agent collaboration — agents handle execution at scale while people retain judgment, approvals, and oversight. Every workflow keeps humans in the loop where it matters.
